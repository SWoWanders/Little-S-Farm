🌾 Little Steve's Farm

You're Steve — the little farmer in the yellow straw hat and blue shirt, with a bright white halo around him so he's always easy to spot. He starts on the dirt path between the crop field and the farmhouse.

Move: WASD / Arrow keys, the joystick (bottom-left) on touch, or just click anywhere on the farm to walk there.

Act: Press E, or tap the DO button, when you're standing close to something.

To plant: tap a seed icon in the tool row (top of screen) to select it, walk into the fenced field just north of you, stand next to a dirt plot, and press E. Same plot again with the 💧 can waters it, and ✂️ shears harvest it once it's grown.

To pet or feed: select ✋ (pet) or 🌾 (feed) from the tool row, walk up to any animal, and press E.

Eggs appear on the ground in the chicken coop yard when a hen lays one — walk right up to an egg and press E to pick it up. Milk comes from a cow once its udder has grown to its biggest stage — press E right next to that cow.

Watering needs a full bucket first — select the watering can 💧 and press E at the pond (the blue water east of the pens), then use it on your plots.

The roadside stand is the red-awning stall south of the field, right by the road — walk up and press E to buy seeds/feed or sell your goods.

The current tool is always shown at the bottom of the screen. Talk to the goat (pet him) — he has opinions.
